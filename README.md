a simple local multiplayer fighting game where two mages duel! inpsired by super smash bros.

each round each player is given a random weapon! weapons will deal damage and knockback to the other player (and sometimes yourself), and the more damage a player takes, the more knockback they receive.

you can also create a magic shield bubble.

both players also have mana, which is drained from shielding and attacking, and passively regenerated

## controls!

either the two players play on one keyboard, or one player plays on keyboard and the other on a controller 

|  | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Movement  | WASD  | Arrow Keys or Controller D-pad |
| Jump | Space  | M or Controller A |
| Attack | F or LMB  | N or Controller B |

you can SHIELD by holding the downwards movement direction.
